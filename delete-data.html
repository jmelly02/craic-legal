Delete your Craic data

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Delete your account and data

Craic doesn't have an in-app "Delete account" button yet. To delete your account:

  1. Email shop@mellyindustries.com with the subject "Craic account deletion".
  2. Send it from the email address on your Craic account, or include that address and your username. If you use Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email", include your Craic username.
  3. We may reply to confirm the request is yours. We'll process it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email, or your Sign in with Apple link) and your profile: username, display name, bio, profile picture.
  • Your contact fields and everything you've shared with friends, plus your friendships and friend requests.
  • Your check-ins, posts, photos/videos, views, ratings, chat and direct messages, and live music listings that still exist. Most of these are already deleted automatically within 2 hours to 7 days.
  • Your live location and location sharing settings.
  • Your calendar events and detected events.
  • Your Facebook link (your app-specific Facebook ID) and linked Facebook login.

What may remain

  • Shared bar information, such as bars added from Apple Maps or a Facebook Page linked to a bar, stays in Craic without your account attached.
  • Anonymous aggregates, such as a bar's 6-hour color history, which expire on their own.
  • Limited backups and security logs kept by our hosting provider, which are deleted on their normal schedule. Anything we must keep by law.

Delete only some data

  • Contact details / sharing: Me → edit or remove fields; on a friend's profile, change "What I share with them"; or unfriend.
  • Live location: Me → Location sharing → off (deleted immediately).
  • Live music you added: on the bar's profile, tap the trash icon next to your listing.
  • Facebook: Friends → Find friends from Facebook → ••• → Disconnect Facebook.
  • For anything else, email us and tell us what you'd like deleted.

Remove Craic from Facebook

If you connected Facebook, you can also remove Craic's access on Facebook's side:

  1. In the Facebook app or on facebook.com, open Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and websites (on newer versions you'll find it under Your activity or the Accounts Center).
  3. Find Craic and choose Remove.

Removing Craic on Facebook stops future access. To also delete the Facebook ID we stored, use Disconnect Facebook in the app or email us.
